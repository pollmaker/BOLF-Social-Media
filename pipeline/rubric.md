# Remake rubric

Each candidate scores 1–5 on four criteria, total out of 20. Keep the criteria fixed
so scores stay comparable between runs.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook** — would the idea stop a scroll today? | Generic topic, no tension | Familiar story with some pull | Built-in tension or curiosity (underdog, mystery, a hidden point of view) |
| **Visual gain** — how much do new AI visuals add? | Already modern, or a talking head that needs no visuals | Dated but watchable | Dated, low-budget or static; scenes people have only imagined |
| **Room to differentiate** — can it be clearly ours? | Only works as a copy | A new angle exists but is thin | Many fresh angles: new point of view, new context, series potential |
| **Channel fit** — does it serve our audience and ministry? | Off-mission | Adjacent | Core to the channel |

## Exclusions (score 0, drop)

- Music videos and lyric videos: the song is the product.
- Personal testimonies and documentaries about real people: the person's story is theirs.
- Secular motivational compilations, fairy tales and off-topic search noise.
- Stories that are someone's original fiction: take the format only, never the plot or characters.

## Rule for everything kept

Remake the idea, never the footage, script or audio. Write our own script from the
source text (scripture, history), add our own narration, and generate our own visuals.
Disclose realistic AI-generated content when uploading.
