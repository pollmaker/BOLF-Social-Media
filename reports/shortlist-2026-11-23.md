## Funnel

Of the 79, about half are search noise: fairy tales, horror cartoons, LEGO and secular
motivational speeches. Dropped under the rubric's exclusions: music and lyric videos,
testimonies and documentaries about real people, stories from other faiths, and kids-only
series (made-for-kids videos lose comments and other features). The remaining videos
collapse into the **15 ideas** below, with duplicate topics merged.
